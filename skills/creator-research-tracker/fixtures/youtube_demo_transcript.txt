The central point of AI infrastructure scaling is that frontier model progress is no longer only a lab story. It is an industrial, supply chain, power, and compute governance story.
If the capability curve continues, the binding constraints become clusters, power availability, networking, memory, deployment speed, and the ability of institutions to react before the model is already widely deployed.
This should be treated as a model candidate, not as a fact: the timing and slope need outside verification from compute buildout, lab roadmaps, company disclosures, and policy evidence.
