The central point of situational awareness is that scaling frontier models is not only a lab story. It is an industrial, national security, and compute governance story.
If the capability curve continues, the binding constraints become clusters, power, talent concentration, export controls, and the ability of institutions to react before the model is already deployed.
This should be treated as a model candidate, not as a fact: the timing and slope need outside verification from compute buildout, lab roadmaps, and policy evidence.
