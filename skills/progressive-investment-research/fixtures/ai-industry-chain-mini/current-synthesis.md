# Current Model / 当前模型：AI Industry Chain Mini

## 当前一句话判断

In this demo model, the AI value chain is analyzed as a system that converts scarce physical supply, capital spending, model capability, and workflow adoption into durable profit pools. The provisional view is that near-term evidence is clearest in physical compute supply, cloud infrastructure, and power/data-center delivery, while model and application layers require stronger proof of retained gross margin and workflow-level pricing power.

## 我们知道什么

- Physical constraints matter: accelerators, memory, advanced packaging, power delivery, cooling, and speed-to-power can each become the binding constraint at different points.
- CapEx recovery is not a yes/no bubble question. It depends on utilization, customer-backed demand, contract duration, depreciation, financing structure, and whether AI-specific revenue can cover capital charges.
- Workflow applications should be judged by whether AI enters a system of record or system of action, not by whether a product claims to use AI.
- Research evidence should be split into source leads, claim rows, number rows, conflicts, and model patch candidates before it changes the model.

## 我们不确定什么

- Whether model/API providers can retain operating surplus after compute cost, sales cost, safety/compliance cost, and platform distribution pressure.
- Whether AI infrastructure supply remains scarce long enough to sustain excess returns.
- Whether enterprise workflow AI creates incremental revenue or becomes bundled functionality inside existing platforms.
- Which company-level disclosures can be normalized into comparable AI-specific revenue, margin, backlog, or work-unit metrics.

## 当前冲突

- Scarcity vs cyclicality: near-term bottlenecks can turn into overcapacity if supply expands faster than realized demand.
- Price compression vs demand expansion: lower inference prices can compress per-token economics while expanding total usage.
- Model capability vs workflow monetization: stronger models do not automatically create durable customer willingness to pay.
