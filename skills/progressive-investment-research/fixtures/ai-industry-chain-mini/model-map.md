# Model Map / 模型地图：AI Industry Chain Mini

## 研究边界

- Includes: AI value-chain structure, physical bottlenecks, CapEx recovery, cloud/model/application monetization, and evidence rules.
- Excludes: short-term trading calls, complete valuation models, private-company financial claims without source boundaries, and precise market-share rankings.
- Source boundary: public-demo fixture. All numbers are illustrative unless explicitly replaced by source-backed rows.

## 核心问题

Which parts of the AI industry chain can retain durable profit as model capability improves, inference prices fall, infrastructure supply expands, and AI becomes embedded in enterprise workflows?

## 分析轴

- Physical supply: accelerator, memory, packaging, power, cooling, data-center delivery.
- Capital recovery: CapEx, leases, commitments, utilization, backlog, AI-specific revenue.
- Monetization: API, seat, work unit, workflow completion, cloud consumption, services.
- Durability: switching cost, data/permission boundary, compliance responsibility, distribution.
- Evidence quality: primary disclosure, vendor snapshot, licensed secondary, press lead, user local file, agent inference.
