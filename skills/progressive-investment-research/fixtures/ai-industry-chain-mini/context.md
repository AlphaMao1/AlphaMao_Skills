# Context

This fixture demonstrates the current dossier contract with a compact AI industry-chain example.

It is intentionally sanitized. The content is illustrative and should not be treated as investment advice, a current market view, or a factual database.

## Default Skill

`progressive-investment-research`

## Recovery Entry

Start with `current-synthesis.md`, then read `model-map.md`, `open-questions.md`, and the modules or models referenced by the current question.

## Write Policy

- Update `current-synthesis.md` only when the model changes.
- Keep detailed evidence and calculations in `modules/` or `models/`.
- Record material changes in `update-log.md`.
- Do not treat search snippets, news summaries, or this fixture's illustrative numbers as model facts.
